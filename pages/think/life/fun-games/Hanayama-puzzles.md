# Puzzles I've tried
## Hanayama Cast Tube Puzzle
- [[#Level 5 - Expert]]
- I solved this one by myself.
- [This YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NRLUIre_Xz8) proposes my solution as "the solution" 
- ...but Alec (AT) told me about an alternative solution, although we struggled to replicate it in the pub. [This video](https://youtube.com/shorts/zT4EGq-GlVY?si=Y-vmBH0lQWJip9W1) suggests a similar technique, not quite the same, but I couldn't get this one to work anyway... 
- ...but finally I found [THIS video](https://youtube.com/shorts/o1M9m31whTI?si=1cWbr_B2rJCS6lkU), which is a slightly different solution, but works consistently every time, and is v satisfying.

- [[#Level 4 - Hard]]
- Andy Stone (AT) lent one of these to me.
- I liked it so much, my plan is to get one for myself
